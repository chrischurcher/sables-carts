Step by step

How it works

  1. Pick your dates and your cart

    You see what's free and what it costs straight away.

  2. We confirm on WhatsApp

    Your 20% non-refundable booking deposit counts towards rent. Your booking confirmation is separate from your payment receipt. Read and accept the full rental agreement and risk acknowledgement online before payment.

  3. We bring it to your door

    Pay the remaining rental balance at delivery in cash or with our approved card terminal. The separate MXN7,191.20 security deposit per cart (about US$400) (cash or temporary card hold at delivery) is arranged near handover. We collect the cart when you are done.

At your door

Easy handover. Clear return.

English

  1. Agree online. Read the agreement, risk acknowledgement, privacy notice and rental details, then tick the acceptance box before paying. Keep your confirmation and agreement copy. No extra paper signature at delivery.
  2. Check together. We note the cart, key, charger, accessories and existing marks. Add any comments. Ordinary wear and recorded existing damage are not charged.
  3. Return and deposit. Return at the agreed place and time for a brief check. If there is no new attributable damage or missing item, we return the full cash deposit of MXN7,191.20 per cart (about US$400) or request release of the full card hold at that check and give you a receipt or confirmation. Your bank controls when a card hold disappears. Any issue needs evidence and an itemised amount; unused security is returned or released without unjustified delay.

Español

  1. Acepte en línea. Lea el contrato, reconocimiento de riesgos, aviso de privacidad y datos de renta; después marque la casilla de aceptación antes de pagar. Conserve la confirmación y copia del contrato. Sin otra firma en papel a la entrega.
  2. Revisamos juntos. Anotamos carrito, llave, cargador, accesorios y marcas existentes. Añada sus observaciones. No cobramos desgaste normal ni daños ya registrados.
  3. Devolución y garantía. Devuelva en el lugar y hora acordados para una revisión breve. Sin daño nuevo atribuible ni accesorios faltantes, devolvemos todo el depósito en efectivo de MXN7,191.20 (aprox. USD400) por carrito o solicitamos liberar toda la retención en tarjeta en esa revisión y le damos recibo o confirmación. Su banco determina cuándo deja de aparecer la retención. Una incidencia requiere evidencia e importe desglosado; devolvemos o liberamos la parte no utilizada sin demora injustificada.

Questions

Good to know

Short answers to what renters usually ask. Anything else, message Chris.

Is delivery really free?

Yes. Delivery and pick-up are free anywhere inside the Puerto Aventuras gates.

When do you deliver?

How does the security deposit work?

Who can drive?

Only named, approved drivers meeting the age, licence and insurance requirements disclosed before booking. No driving after drinking.

Where can I take it?

Anywhere inside the gates. Not on Highway 307 and not on the beach. The speed limit inside is 30 km/h, and lights go on at night.

Does it need charging?

It's electric. Plug it in overnight and it's ready in the morning. Keep the charger locked to the cart or indoors.

What if something goes wrong?

Message Chris on WhatsApp. He fixes it or swaps the cart.

Can I cancel?

The 20% booking deposit is non-refundable.

Staying a month or longer?

The monthly price applies automatically, and it's the best rate we have. Pick your dates above and you'll see it.

Sable runs the place

Sable is the owner, the supervisor and the overseer. Chris only checks the carts, charges them and brings them to your door.
