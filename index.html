Good to know

Is delivery really free?

Yes. Delivery and pick-up are free anywhere inside the Puerto Aventuras gates.

When do you deliver?

How does the security hold work?

Who can drive?

Only named, approved drivers meeting the age, licence and insurance requirements disclosed before booking. No driving after drinking.

Where can I take it?

Anywhere inside the gates. Not on Highway 307 and not on the beach. The speed limit inside is 30 km/h, and lights go on at night.

Does it need charging?

It's electric. Plug it in overnight and it's ready in the morning. Keep the charger locked to the cart or indoors.

What if something goes wrong?

Message Chris on WhatsApp. He fixes it or swaps the cart.

Can I cancel?

The 20% booking deposit is non-refundable.

Staying a month or longer?

The monthly price applies automatically, and it's the best rate we have. Pick your dates above and you'll see it.

Sable runs the place

Sable is the owner, the supervisor and the overseer. Chris only checks the carts, charges them and brings them to your door.
